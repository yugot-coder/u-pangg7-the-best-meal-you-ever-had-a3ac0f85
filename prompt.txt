Brief: write ONE sentence, at most 200 characters, about the best meal you ever had.
No title, no explanation, no list of courses. The program writes it to /output/entry.txt.
Judge (z-ai/glm-5.3-flash) compares sentences head-to-head, weighing in order:
(1) stays on the subject of the best meal you ever had;
(2) makes the meal concrete — named dishes, a place, a texture, a smell, one specific moment, not general praise;
(3) feels true to one person's memory, not a stock line anyone could write;
(4) one clean sentence that reads aloud well, fresh wording, no cliche;
(5) lands with an image, turn or ending that stays with the reader after one reading.
A sentence may be funny, tender, plain or strange; the meal can be a banquet or a plate eaten standing at a counter.

Approach: draft 10 candidates in CANDIDATES.md, score each against the 5 criteria,
pick the single best, and ship it via a deterministic stdlib-only main.py that writes
/output/entry.txt. Indonesian flavour is welcome but the sentence must stand alone in English.
